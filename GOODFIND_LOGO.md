# GOOD FIND WEAR — LOGO SYSTEM

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Purpose:** Source of truth for Good Find Wear logo usage in profile, carousel, landing page, and brand visuals.

## 1. PRIMARY LOGO — LOCKED

Use the **Clean Minimal GOODFIND WEAR wordmark (Option 02)** as the primary brand logo.

Direction:
- Clean
- Minimal
- Fashion-editorial
- Mature but approachable
- Strong readability at small sizes
- Black/charcoal on light backgrounds
- Reversed light version when used on dark backgrounds

Do not redraw, reinterpret, or replace the primary logo with an unrelated typeface or AI-generated logo.

## 2. SECONDARY MARK — PROFILE PIC / COMPACT USE

Use the **GF monogram (Option 03)** as the official secondary brand mark.

Primary use:
- Instagram profile picture
- Favicon
- Small watermark
- Compact brand mark
- Small-space applications

The GF monogram should be treated as a signature mark for Good Find Wear.

## 3. EXPRESSIVE / CAMPAIGN LOGO

The original playful handwritten **GoodFind Wear** logo remains an expressive/campaign variant.

Use selectively for:
- Lifestyle/campaign content
- Community-oriented visuals
- Stickers/merch concepts
- Occasional brand moments

It is not the default primary logo for standard carousel layouts.

## 4. CAROUSEL LOGO RULE — NON-NEGOTIABLE

**EVERY GOOD FIND WEAR CAROUSEL SLIDE MUST CONTAIN A GOOD FIND WEAR LOGO / APPROVED BRAND MARK.**

Default treatment:
- Place the approved logo/mark in the **top-left area** of each slide.
- Keep placement visually consistent across the full 5-slide carousel.
- Use the primary wordmark when there is enough space.
- Use the GF monogram only when the layout is too tight for the full wordmark.
- On dark photography, use the approved reversed/light treatment for legibility.
- On light/cream backgrounds, use the approved dark treatment.

The logo must remain readable and must not compete with the main headline or product.

## 5. LOGO PROTECTION

Never:
- Change the logo font/style
- Recreate the logo with a different typeface
- Stretch or distort the mark
- Change its proportions
- Add shadows, gradients, 3D effects, or unnecessary effects
- Place it over busy/high-detail areas without sufficient contrast
- Crop the logo
- Cover or merge the logo with other graphic elements
- Invent new recurring logo variations without explicit approval

Maintain clear space around the logo and keep the treatment consistent across the feed.

## 6. PROFILE PICTURE

**Official profile picture direction: GF monogram (Option 03).**

The profile mark should be optimized for a square/circular social avatar and remain recognizable at small size.

## 7. VISUAL PRIORITY

When logo usage conflicts with layout aesthetics:

**Brand consistency > decorative variety.**

The logo is mandatory, but it should be integrated cleanly into the editorial layout rather than treated like a loud sales badge.

## 8. RELATED BRAND SYSTEM

Primary wordmark: **Clean Minimal GOODFIND WEAR (Option 02)**  
Secondary mark: **GF Monogram (Option 03)**  
Expressive mark: **Original playful handwritten GoodFind Wear logo**  
Tagline: **Good stuff. Good looks.**

> **ONE BRAND. THREE ROLES. CONSISTENTLY RECOGNIZABLE.**
